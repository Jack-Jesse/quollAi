## Current public description

Creating Villages facilitates Cafe QB with Quantin Binnah Community Centre. Its 2025 ACNC filing states that the cafe is run by volunteers, provides work-skills training to people with disability and hosts community groups intended to reduce social isolation. Current registered programs include Craft and Caffeine craft cafe, Craft Ability, Craft Therapy, Ooey gOOey messy play, High Tea and Craft, Lunch with a Bunch and Homeschool Cooking.

## Private design briefs

### 1. The Open Table

- Audience and goal: local adults and families, convert to a drop-in visit
- Theme and accent: light, tangerine
- Radius: generous 28px family
- Type: bold geometric display with clean humanist body
- Composition: split hero, direct weekly hours, full-width community photography
- Memorable idea: an orange open-door frame around the cafe image

### 2. Paper Garden

- Audience and goal: craft-led visitors, convert to an email enquiry
- Theme and accent: warm light, raspberry
- Radius: soft 18px family
- Type: expressive serif display with compact sans body
- Composition: editorial collage, oversized craft image, paper-like section layering
- Memorable idea: flower-colour blocks that feel cut from card stock

### 3. The Community Board

- Audience and goal: practical local visitors, find a program and current update quickly
- Theme and accent: dark navy, golden yellow
- Radius: tight 6px family
- Type: condensed uppercase display with utilitarian sans body
- Composition: high-energy poster hero, schedule rail, stacked program directory
- Memorable idea: the site behaves like a legible community noticeboard

### 4. A Quiet Cup

- Audience and goal: partners, supporters and new visitors, understand the mission and call
- Theme and accent: warm ivory, cobalt
- Radius: near-square 2px family
- Type: high-contrast editorial serif with restrained sans body
- Composition: centred statement, gallery-like image bands, spacious facts
- Memorable idea: one calm blue rule guides the reading rhythm

### 5. Village Table

- Audience and goal: volunteers and community-minded visitors, convert to participation
- Theme and accent: deep green, coral
- Radius: organic 40px family
- Type: friendly rounded display with understated sans body
- Composition: photo mosaic hero, conversational sections, prominent contact close
- Memorable idea: overlapping images form one shared table
